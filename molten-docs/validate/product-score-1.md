## Red flags

- 🟡 **Solution-first pitch** — The idea was introduced as "ReefTracker, a web app for X/Y/Z" rather than starting from a customer and a problem. Worth checking whether the product shape is driving the problem definition, rather than the other way round.
- 🟡 **A free/cheap incumbent likely covers most of the workflow** — You said apps already exist for recording and charting water parameters, just "underdeveloped or not maintained." If those apps already cover ~80% of what hobbyists need, the bar for ReefTracker to be worth switching to is higher than "nicer version."

Three flags is the threshold for "stop and reassess" — combined with the low total score, that's the basis for the Kill verdict above.
